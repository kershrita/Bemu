# Version 1 release notes

Version 1 makes the whole hexapod walk in simulation. Nothing is bought yet.

## What you can do now

- Stand and sit the full 18-joint robot (MuJoCo), headless or with a window.
- Walk it with three gaits (tripod, ripple, wave), forward, sideways and turning in place, while holding body height, shift and tilt commands. Switching gait while walking blends over two cycles without stopping.
- Do all of that without ROS (`python3 tools/walk.py`) or through ROS 2: `ros2 launch bemu_bringup robot.launch.py` starts the simulator, controllers and walker; it stands up by itself and stops 0.5 s after the last `/cmd_vel`.
- Drive it by hand: `ros2 run bemu_teleop keyboard`, or `robot.launch.py teleop:=gamepad`.
- Regenerate the torque report: `python3 tools/torque_report.py` (about 75 s).

## Results

All six roadmap exit tests pass (headless, in CI). The torque report (48 simulated runs: a stand, then every gait forward at three speeds, sideways and turning, at 2.0, 2.2 and 2.5 kg) passes the servo rule in every row: worst peak 1.26 N·m (43% of the Feetech STS3215 12 V's stall torque, limit 50%), worst RMS 0.43 N·m (44% of rated), no step at the 1.47 N·m design limit, stability margin never below 69.8 mm.

## New packages

`bemu_gait` (gaits and the walker), `bemu_msgs` (`GaitCommand`, `GaitState`), `bemu_locomotion` (the walker node), `bemu_teleop` (keyboard and gamepad), plus the full-robot model in `bemu_description`, collisions and statistics in `bemu_sim`, and `tools/{stand,walk,torque_report}.py`.

## Known limitations

- **Foot slip while changing gait.** A planted foot can slide up to about 25 mm while the walker blends from one gait to another (steady walking stays within 5 mm). The proper fix is to lift a foot only when it is safe to; it is left for a later version.
- **Tripod sideways** slips about 6 mm at top speed.
- **Torque peaks are simulator transients.** They barely change with total mass and come from the simulated servo loop (kp 20, kv 0.5), not from carrying the body. Version 2's one-leg measurement checks this against the real servo.
- **Speed headroom is thin.** Joint speed reaches 83% of the 4.7 rad/s no-load speed (wave sideways); the loaded speed is lower and unknown.
- **Power estimate:** stall current (2.7 A) and idle current (30 mA, corrected after the release from a 0.1 A placeholder) now come from the servo datasheet; the 12 V supply is nominal, so the real battery voltage will move the figures (about 21–39 W, an upper bound). Version 2 measures.
- `GaitCommand.step_height` and `cycle_time` are reserved; non-zero values are ignored with a warning.
- Keyboard control is latched (a terminal has no key release): keys step the speed, space stops, and it runs in its own terminal.
- The simulated launch tests (`-m sim_launch`) run on the development PC only, not in CI.
