# Version 1.5 release notes

Version 1.5 turns the simulated robot into parts that can be ordered, and a list of what to buy.
Nothing is bought yet: Purchase gate 1 is the owner's decision.

## What you can do now

- Regenerate every printed part, its STEP and STL files, the printability report and the print order:
  `python3 tools/generate_cad.py` (output in `cad/export/`; `--sync-masses` also writes the CAD
  masses and inertias into `config/robot_params.yaml`).
- Look at the robot as the CAD parts: `ros2 launch bemu_bringup robot.launch.py` renders the same
  meshes in MuJoCo and RViz as the parts to be printed (30 STL files, about 5.9 MB, in
  `bemu_description/meshes`). Preview images: `python3 tools/render_preview.py`.
- Check any component's data and its source: `config/components.yaml` (every value has a source and
  a status: datasheet, cad, standard, measured, unverified).
- Order the first print: the tolerance coupon (`cad/export/tolerance_coupon.stl`), then follow
  `docs/reports/v1_5-purchase-list.md` and send `docs/reports/v1_5-print-quote-request.md`.

## Known limitations and open items

- **Fit-critical servo values are still unverified**: the bracket-hole thread (M2 assumed), case
  shelf positions, horn disc diameter and height (9.2 mm in the Feetech STEP, 9.6 mm in the
  Waveshare drawing; 9.6 is used), rear idler and hub. The print order says NOT READY TO ORDER until
  they are measured on the first servo and the tolerance coupon is measured.
- **The pocket allowance** (how much bigger holes and pockets print) is an assumption until the coupon
  is measured; the insert hole (4.1 mm) and insert length (4 mm) are unverified too.
- **Electronics trays, hard stops and cable slots are not designed**: the Pi, battery and regulator
  must be bought and measured first. Their masses are placeholders (575 g).
- **The mass reserve is small**: about 86 g remains under the 2.5 kg design mass for add-on modules.
- **Not analysed**: stiffness of the coxa support plate, collisions between neighbouring legs at
  extreme poses; weigh the finished parts and robot (version 3).
- **The simulator's body collision box** keeps the 240 × 160 mm footprint (only the thickness was
  synced to the CAD).
- **Prices are listing snapshots** from 2026-11-19 searches, several not priced at all, and shipping to
  Saudi Arabia, VAT and duty were not confirmed. The customs notes are unverified. Buy the battery
  inside Saudi Arabia.
- **Not yet run on the owner's PC**: the CAD-mesh robot in `robot.launch.py` (pull, `colcon build
  --symlink-install`, launch).
- **Carried from version 1:** foot slip while blending gaits (about 25 mm), torque peaks are simulator
  transients, thin speed headroom.

## For the owner, in order

1. Pull the branch, rebuild, launch `robot.launch.py`, confirm the CAD robot renders and walks.
2. Send `docs/reports/v1_5-print-quote-request.md` to two or three print services; order the coupon
   alone; measure it.
3. Approve (or change) `docs/reports/v1_5-purchase-list.md`; order four servos and the bench items.
4. When the first servo arrives, caliper it, check its screws, enter the values in
   `config/components.yaml`; the print order then says READY TO ORDER.
